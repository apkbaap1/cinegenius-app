## The story in three sentences
1. A monkey in a jamun tree by the river throws sweet jamun fruit to a crocodile every day, and they become friends.
2. The crocodile's wife wants the monkey's heart, so the crocodile carries the monkey across the river on his back — and in the middle of the river he confesses.
3. The monkey says his heart is up in the tree, gets carried back, leaps to safety, and the friendship ends.

**Transformation**: trust → wit. **Turn**: mid-river confession (~ 0:40). **Token**: one jamun fruit — thrown
in friendship at the start, and at the end left floating alone on the empty river.
